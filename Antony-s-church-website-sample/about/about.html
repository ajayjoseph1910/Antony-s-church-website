OUR CHURCH

About St. Antony's Church

St. Antony's Church is a spiritual home for families, children, young people and everyone who seeks God's presence in their lives.

Through Holy Mass, prayer, sacraments, community activities and charitable service, the church continues to encourage people to live according to the teachings of Jesus Christ.

Our parish community is united by faith and guided by the values of love, compassion, forgiveness and service. The church welcomes everyone to grow in faith and become part of a loving Christian community.
